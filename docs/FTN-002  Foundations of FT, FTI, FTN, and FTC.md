# FTN-002 - Foundations of FT, FTI, FTN, and FTC
## Four Core Concepts

The Function Tunnel framework is built upon four progressively larger concepts:

    FT
    ↓
    FTI
    ↓
    FTN
    ↓
    FTC

## FT: Function Tunnel

A Function Tunnel is a stable functional pathway that supports a particular category of perception, reasoning, decision making, or action.

A Function Tunnel is not a piece of knowledge.

Instead, it is a reusable operational structure.

Examples include:

- mathematical reasoning pathways,
- musical perception pathways,
- software debugging pathways,
- scientific hypothesis generation pathways.

## FTI: Function Tunnel Intelligence

Function Tunnel Intelligence describes the ability to:

- construct tunnels,
- maintain tunnels,
- repair tunnels,
- connect tunnels,
- and evolve tunnels.

From this perspective, intelligence is not unrestricted optimization.

It is the ability to operate effectively within and across functional pathways.

## FTN: Function Tunnel Networks

Individual tunnels rarely exist in isolation.

As tunnels accumulate, they form interconnected networks.

These networks support:

- transfer learning,
- cross-domain reasoning,
- creativity,
- specialization,
- collective coordination.

FTN represents the structural ecosystem of intelligence.

## FTC: Function Tunnel Capital

Function Tunnel Capital represents the accumulated value of Function Tunnel Networks.

FTC determines:

- future learning efficiency,
- problem-solving capacity,
- adaptability,
- and long-term comparative advantage.

FTC functions similarly to economic capital, but operates within the domain of intelligence and capability formation.

## Knowledge Feeds FT but Is Not FT

One of the central hypotheses of this work is:

> Knowledge feeds Function Tunnels, but knowledge is not Function Tunnels.

Knowledge provides material.

Function Tunnels provide structure.

Without tunnels, knowledge often remains isolated information.

With tunnels, knowledge becomes operational capability.

## A Structural View of Human Development

Under the FTN framework:

- learning becomes tunnel formation,
- expertise becomes tunnel stabilization,
- creativity becomes tunnel recombination,
- wisdom becomes tunnel network optimization.
