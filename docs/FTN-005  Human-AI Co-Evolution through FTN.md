# FTN-005 - Human-AI Co-Evolution through Function Tunnel Networks

## Beyond Tool Usage

Most discussions describe AI as a tool.

The FTN framework suggests a different perspective.

AI may become a participant in Function Tunnel Networks.

## Human-AI Shared FTNs

Human-AI collaboration can produce shared FTNs.

Examples include:

- scientific research,
- engineering design,
- software development,
- education,
- creative production.

The resulting network may exceed either participant alone.

## The Obot-and-Me Pattern

Long-term Human-AI collaboration demonstrates that repeated interaction can create stable shared tunnels.

Over time:

- communication becomes more efficient,
- context becomes richer,
- reasoning becomes more aligned,
- productivity increases.

The partnership itself becomes a new FTN.

## AI Brain Units

Future AI systems may evolve toward specialized Brain Units.

Different AI units may accumulate distinct FTC profiles.

Collective intelligence may emerge through cooperation among heterogeneous AI-Brain Units.
