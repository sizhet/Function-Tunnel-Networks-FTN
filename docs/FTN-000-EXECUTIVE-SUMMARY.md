# FTN-000 - Executive Summary
## Function Tunnel Networks (FTN)
### A Structural Perspective on Intelligence, Education, Division of Labor, and Human-AI Co-Evolution

![./figures/Fig-001-Position-of-FTN-in-Structural-Intelligence.png](./figures/Fig-001-Position-of-FTN-in-Structural-Intelligence.png)

## Why This Repository?

Many theories explain intelligence through:

- knowledge,
- memory,
- information,
- optimization,
- learning algorithms.

These explanations are valuable.

However, they do not fully explain:

- why individuals develop different capabilities,
- why expertise compounds,
- why specialization emerges,
- why educational outcomes differ dramatically,
- why Human-AI collaboration can become increasingly productive.

This repository explores a structural hypothesis:

> Intelligence grows through the formation of stable functional pathways.

We call these pathways Function Tunnels (FT).

## Four Core Concepts

    FT
    ↓
    FTI
    ↓
    FTN
    ↓
    FTC
    
### FT

Function Tunnel

A reusable functional pathway.

### FTI

Function Tunnel Intelligence

The ability to create, repair, connect, and evolve Function Tunnels.

### FTN

Function Tunnel Networks

Networks formed by interacting Function Tunnels.

### FTC

Function Tunnel Capital

Accumulated capability derived from Function Tunnel Networks.

## Key Hypotheses
### Knowledge Feeds FT but Is Not FT

Knowledge provides material.

Function Tunnels provide operational structure.

### Education Is Tunnel Engineering

The purpose of education is not merely content delivery.

It is the construction of functional pathways.

### Division of Labor Emerges from FTN

Professional specialization and comparative advantage arise from differences in FTC.

### Human-AI Collaboration Creates Shared FTNs

Human-AI partnerships may evolve into shared Function Tunnel Networks.

### Career Selection Is FTC Selection

In the AI era, career choices increasingly become decisions about Function Tunnel Capital.

## Status

FTN is an open research framework.

It is not a finished theory.

The repository is intended as a platform for discussion, criticism, refinement, and future research.
