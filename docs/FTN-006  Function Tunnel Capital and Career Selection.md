# FTN-006 - Function Tunnel Capital and Career Selection
## Choosing Paths in the AI Era

## The Career Selection Problem

The AI era challenges many traditional assumptions about careers.

The question is no longer:

    "What job is popular?"

A more important question may be:

    "What Function Tunnel Capital am I building?"

## FTC Maximization

Career decisions should consider:

- tunnel growth potential,
- tunnel transferability,
- tunnel durability,
- tunnel connectivity,
- Human-AI complementarity.
- High-Value FTC

## High-value FTC often possesses:

- long-term relevance,
- strong transferability,
- cross-domain applicability,
- AI amplification potential.

## Low-Value FTC

Low-value FTC may rely heavily on:

- repetitive procedures,
- narrow context,
- low adaptability,
- weak transferability.

Such FTC may become increasingly vulnerable to automation.

## Human Agency in the AI Era

Human agency does not disappear.

Instead, agency shifts toward:

- selecting tunnels,
- cultivating tunnels,
- connecting tunnels,
- evolving tunnel networks.

FTC management may become one of the most important life skills of the future.
